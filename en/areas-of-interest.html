Clinical Areas of Interest

Areas of Interest

The main areas my clinical and academic work focuses on are listed below.

01

Pediatric Urology

Evaluation and treatment of hypospadias, undescended testis, vesicoureteral reflux, congenital urinary tract anomalies, and other urologic conditions in childhood.

02

Robotic Urology

Robot-assisted urologic approaches that combine three-dimensional imaging, precise instrument movement, and minimally invasive surgical principles.

03

Reconstructive Urology

Urethral strictures, complex urinary tract reconstruction, function-preserving surgery, and tissue repair approaches.

04

Endourology & Stone Surgery

Percutaneous nephrolithotomy, flexible ureterorenoscopy, laser stone surgery, and other minimally invasive stone treatments.

05 — Secondary Areas

Secondary academic and clinical areas of interest
