Cookie Policy

Use of Cookies

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies that track visitor behavior. The site only uses the local storage described below for a technical purpose.

Local Storage Used

A single preference value is stored in your browser's localStorage so it can remember your dark/light mode preference. This data stays on your device, is never sent to a server, and is not used to identify you.

Third-Party Cookies

The site does not include any third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) or advertising cookies. The form service (Formspree) used once the contact form is configured may be subject to its own cookie policy.

Managing Your Preferences

You can clear local storage from your browser settings at any time; doing so will reset your dark/light mode preference.
