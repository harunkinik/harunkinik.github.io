About Me

Abdullah Harun Kınık, MD, completed his medical degree at Bezmialem Vakıf University School of Medicine and his urology residency at the University of Health Sciences Gaziosmanpaşa Training and Research Hospital. His clinical work focuses primarily on pediatric urology, robotic and minimally invasive surgery, and reconstructive urology.

He trained clinically in pediatric and robotic urology under Prof. Mesrur Selçuk Sılay, MD. He later completed a clinical training period in pediatric and robotic urology under Prof. Anne-Françoise Spinoit, MD, at Ghent University in Belgium, as part of the European Urology Scholarship Programme.

Dr. Kınık has published scientific work in national and international peer-reviewed journals and continues research in pediatric urology, surgical outcomes assessment, AI in urology, and medical education. He is an Associate Member of the Young Academic Urologists Pediatric Urology Working Group within the European Association of Urology, and previously served on the Turkish Board of the European Society of Residents in Urology.

His approach to patient care is grounded in current scientific evidence, thorough evaluation, and open communication.
