What Is KVKK?

This website is operated from Turkey and is governed by Turkish law, regardless of the language a visitor reads it in. KVKK — the Law on the Protection of Personal Data (Law No. 6698) — is Turkey's personal data protection legislation, broadly comparable to the EU's GDPR. It sets out how personal data may be collected, processed, and stored, and what rights individuals have over their own data.

Data Controller

Under KVKK, the data controller for this website is Abdullah Harun Kınık, MD.

Personal Data Processed

If you fill out the form on the Contact page, your full name, email address, phone number (if provided), and message content are processed. The site has no other mechanism for collecting personal data.

Purpose of Processing

The data listed above is processed solely to respond to the question or request you submit. It is not used for marketing or any other purpose.

Data Transfers

Once configured, the contact form may operate through a third-party form service (Formspree), whose servers may be located outside Turkey. In that case, your data is transmitted through that provider's infrastructure.

Your Rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is being processed, request information about such processing, learn its purpose, know the third parties to whom it is transferred domestically or abroad, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request its deletion or destruction, and object to the processing.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise these rights, you can reach out at info@harunkinik.com.
