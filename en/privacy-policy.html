Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

General

harunkinik.com is the personal and academic website of Abdullah Harun Kınık, MD. This page explains how the site handles visitor data.

Data Collected

This is a static website with no backend server or database. The site only processes your full name, email, phone number (optional), subject, and message when you fill out the form on the Contact page. Once the form is configured, this information is transmitted through a third-party form service (Formspree).

Cookies

Aside from remembering your dark/light mode preference, the site does not use any tracking cookies or analytics tools. See the Cookie Policy page for details.

Third-Party Services

The Formspree service may be used once the contact form is configured. Formspree's own privacy policy applies. The site does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out at info@harunkinik.com.
